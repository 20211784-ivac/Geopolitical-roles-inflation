# Data sources, attribution, and reuse boundary

This code-only repository does not redistribute the merged panel or raw third-party datasets.

## AI-GPR

Source: Matteo Iacoviello and Dario Tong, official AI-GPR project website and associated data/code documentation.

The project archive records an owner-supplied 15-August-2026 snapshot date, but the provider-side extraction timestamp and exact historical vintage are not independently verified. Cite the AI-GPR source and authors in any reuse. Before redistributing any downloaded or transformed AI-GPR files, verify the license text attached to the exact release used and preserve all required attribution.

Official project site: https://www.matteoiacoviello.com/ai_gpr

## UCDP Conflict Termination Dataset v4-2024

Source: Uppsala Conflict Data Program, Department of Peace and Conflict Research, Uppsala University. Cite the dataset version and its associated methodological publication (including Kreutz, 2010, where applicable).

The official UCDP download center identifies CC BY 4.0 as the applicable license for its datasets. Verify the current dataset-specific notice at download and include attribution when reusing or redistributing it.

Download center: https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/index.html

## CPI and inflation series

The project records identify national statistical sources including Statistics Finland, the UK Office for National Statistics, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, and Gabon's INSTAT. Their tables and series may have different reuse conditions. No blanket license is asserted here. Obtain each series from its official source, retain the series/table identifier and retrieval date, and check the provider's reuse terms.

## World Bank / Global Database of Inflation

The merged project panel includes CPI-related data attributed in project documentation to the World Bank Global Database of Inflation. The precise source release and terms for each series must be checked before redistribution. This repository does not include those raw files.

## Derived files

No merged source panel, country-month data extract, frozen prediction archive, or derived microdata is included in this code-only release. If derived outputs are added in a later version, review them for third-party content and disclosure restrictions first.
